Adds purchase orders to the forecast lines generated by
``mis_builder_cash_flow_auto``.

For each line of a confirmed purchase order the amount not yet on a
posted vendor bill (taxes included) is expected to be billed on the
expected arrival date of the line (today if that has passed) and paid by
the payment terms of the order, or of the vendor. The lines are shown on
the payable account of the vendor.

Usage
=====

Nothing to do: update the forecast (*Update Forecast* in the forecast
lines, or wait for the nightly update) and the orders are in the *Out
forecast* rows of the *Cash Flow* MIS report.

Draft bills do not reduce the forecast, because the cash flow report
does not show draft bills either.
