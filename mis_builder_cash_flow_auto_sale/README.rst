Adds confirmed sales orders to the forecast lines generated by
``mis_builder_cash_flow_auto``.

For each confirmed order the amount not yet invoiced (taxes included,
less the posted invoices) is expected to be invoiced on the delivery
date of the order (the commitment date, else the expected date, else the
order date; today if that has passed) and paid by the payment terms of
the order. The lines are shown on the receivable account of the
customer.

Usage
=====

Nothing to do: update the forecast (*Update Forecast* in the forecast
lines, or wait for the nightly update) and the orders are in the *In
forecast* rows of the *Cash Flow* MIS report.

Draft invoices do not reduce the forecast, because the cash flow report
does not show draft invoices either.
