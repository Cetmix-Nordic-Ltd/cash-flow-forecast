Adds the future invoices of OCA contracts (`contract`) to the forecast
lines generated by `mis_builder_cash_flow_auto`.

The invoice dates follow the recurrence of each contract line up to the
forecast horizon and the end date of the line. The amount of a line is
its subtotal with the taxes of its product, mapped by the fiscal
position of the contract. The invoices are paid by the payment terms of
the contract, or of the partner. Customer contracts are money in on the
receivable account, vendor contracts money out on the payable account.
